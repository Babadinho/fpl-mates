# Disclaimer

**fpl-mates is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier
League, Fantasy Premier League, or the Football Association.** All trade marks
belong to their respective owners. No Premier League or club marks, crests, kit
colours or fonts are used anywhere in this project — the design is deliberately
original for that reason.

The project reads from the public Fantasy Premier League JSON API at
`fantasy.premierleague.com/api/`. That API is **undocumented**. It requires no
authentication and has been stable for years, but it carries no guarantees: it
can change shape, start rate-limiting, or disappear entirely, without notice and
without anybody being obliged to tell you. Field names have changed between
seasons before.

**Run this at your own risk.** It is intended for private, non-commercial use by
a group of friends running their own mini-league. Every instance is self-hosted:
you run it, against your own league, on your own infrastructure. There is no
shared service, and nothing here is operated on anyone else's behalf.

If you are thinking of running a hosted version for other people, or charging
for it, that is your decision to make and your terms to check.
